# Videos

## Overview

This folder contains notes, summaries, and study materials created from the video lectures and mini-lessons throughout the MIT Professional Education Data Engineering Program.

The video notes are designed to supplement the course content by providing structured summaries, key concepts, formulas, examples, and practical applications discussed in each lesson.

These notes serve as both a study guide and a long-term reference for future coursework and professional projects.

---

## Purpose

The video notes help:

- Reinforce concepts taught in lectures
- Organize learning materials
- Document important formulas and definitions
- Provide quick review material for assignments and assessments
- Create a searchable knowledge repository
